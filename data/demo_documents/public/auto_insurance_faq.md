# Demo Auto Insurance FAQ

This is fictional sample information for testing an insurance knowledge assistant.

## What is an insurance claim?
An insurance claim is a formal request made by a policyholder to an insurer for coverage of a loss or damage under an insurance policy.

## What information is needed to report an accident?
A policyholder should provide the policy number, vehicle registration number, date of accident, location, and a description of the incident.

## Does reporting a claim guarantee approval?
No. Reporting a claim does not guarantee approval. The insurer reviews the policy terms, submitted documents, and circumstances before making a decision.
