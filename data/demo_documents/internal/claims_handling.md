# Demo Claims Handling Procedure

This is fictional internal sample information.

## Claim registration
Every reported claim must receive a unique claim reference number.

## Initial verification
The claims team checks the policy number, policy status, incident date, and basic incident details.

## Document collection
The team may request photographs, repair estimates, incident reports, and other supporting documents.

## Claim assessment
The claims team reviews the available evidence and applicable policy conditions before deciding the next action.
